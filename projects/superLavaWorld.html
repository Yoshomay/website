Super Lava World

This is a floor is lava styled hack inspried by Kosmic's Super Lava Bros. Generally I removed the ground in all the levels, if the level was impossible (without the cape) I'd take creative liberties to make the level interesting still.

Some of the jumps are annoying to do (eg bouncing off of multiple enemies in a row over a void), so inside Yoshi's house is the yellow switch which acts as an easy mode.

I wanted to literally make the floor lava, but cus of how mario world sprites work I couldn't do that without a ton of pain so I just made most of the levels a bottomless pit, it's whatever.
