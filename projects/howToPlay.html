I can't legally share the original roms of my hacks, or any official Nintendo rom, so you're going to have to obtain a legal copy of Mario World/Mario 64 and patch them to play any of my hacks.

All my rom hacks use the us version, any us mario 64 rom should work, but for the mario world hacks, only roms titled "Super Mario World (U) [!].smc" (specifically .smc) are tested to work.


To patch the roms, first obtain a copy of mario world/mario 64, and download Flips or use the Hack64 Web Patcher.

Flips: Exract the zip file, and open flips, click "Apply Patch". Select the patch you want to use, then select an original mario world/mario 64 rom, now select where you want the rom saved. Now it'll be wherever you specified in the last step, just open it in an emulator/flash cart and enjoy :3

Web Patcher: Click the top browse button, and select an original mario world/mario 64 rom, then click the 2nd browse button and select the patch you want to use, now click patch and the rom will be in your download folder, ready to be used.
