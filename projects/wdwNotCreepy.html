Wet Dry World Not Creepy

Back in 2020 during the "Wet Dry World negative emotional aura" trend, I had the idea of, what would Wet Dry World look like if it wasn't creepy?

Looking back it's nothing that great and isn't even that less creepy, but thats how looking back at your old art is.

Included are 2 patches, "level only" has only the new "not creepy" wdw, whereas "full game" is the full game but with the not creepy version of wdw. Do note however that the full game version still has rom manager's patches applied (such as extend level boundaries) which are known to break vanilla levels.
