Super Mario Bros. World

A remake of Mario 1 in Mario World, with the overworld from Super Mario Bros. Deluxe, unique level themes, and many custom songs to keep the monotonous levels from getting stale. (With a hidden bonus world :3)

This hack was made to be more so "what if Mario World had the levels of Mario 1", rather than a straight mario 1 port into smw's engine.

I first tried to make this hack way back in 2020, the new 2026 version isn't based on and doesn't use anything from the 2020 demo, but here's that if you wanna try it out.
